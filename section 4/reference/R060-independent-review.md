# Remark 4.4: independent review of the comparison map

This is a paper-level argument and dependency analysis, not a completed
formalization of the varieties or graded Grothendieck rings. The natural-map wording is now applied in the working manuscript
under the author's standing authorization for independently established clear
corrections. The geometric counterexample is a paper-level reconstruction,
not a completed Lean counterexample.

## External fact checked

Ito–Miura–Okawa–Ueda, *The class of the affine line is a zero divisor in
the Grothendieck ring: via G₂-Grassmannians*, arXiv:1606.04210v2,
[Theorems 1.1 and 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.04210), give smooth
projective complex threefolds X,Y such that ([X]−[Y])L=0 and with different
stable birational classes. The proof of 1.3 explicitly uses the map from
the ordinary Grothendieck ring modulo L to stable birational classes.
This verifies the external facts cited in R060; it does not by itself prove
the proposed application to the manuscript's graded rings.

## Reconstructed graded argument

Let Rₙ be the group generated by complex constructible sets of dimension
at most n, modulo constructible bijections and scissor relations **inside
that dimension bound**. Keep Rₙ separate from the image of Rₙ in the
ordinary Grothendieck ring; their equality cannot be assumed.

For each n, an additive invariant bₙ sends an object to the sum of the
birational classes of its n-dimensional irreducible components in the free
abelian group on those classes. Constructible bijections preserve it:
restrict their constructible graphs to dense opens of the components.
Scissors preserve it because exactly one piece of a finite constructible
partition contains a dense open in each irreducible component. Pieces of
smaller dimension contribute zero. Thus this is an invariant of Rₙ, with
no cancellation through objects of dimension greater than n.

Write L₁ for the homogeneous affine-line class and
r=[X]₃−[Y]₃. For every m≥0, b₃₊ₘ distinguishes X×Aᵐ and Y×Aᵐ:
otherwise X and Y would be stably birational. Consequently L₁ᵐr≠0
in R₃₊ₘ for every m. The localization criterion then gives
z=r/L₁³≠0 in the degree-zero piece of the graded localization.
The natural comparison sends z to zero in the ordinary localization,
since its numerator is annihilated there by L.

To apply this example to the manuscript, use an algebraically closed
parameter field containing all complex constants and named lifts of its
Gamma elements, for example the full algebraically closed Puiseux field.
For each definable value, translation by a named RV representative identifies
its fibre with Gₘ, so the !I generators already vanish. Stable embeddedness
of the residue field identifies the RES objects and bijections with the
constructible ones above. This last identification must be retained in a
formal counterexample; it is not supplied by a generic ring calculation.

## Conclusion and exact formal boundary

The top-dimensional invariant gives a coherent independent reason that the
claimed injection fails; the natural comparison map still exists. The
proposed amendment is to replace “injective homomorphism” with “natural
homomorphism” in the affine-localized paragraph only. The next,
point-localized isomorphism is a different assertion and is unaffected.

`ReviewCalculations.lean` checks the general localization mechanism:
a numerator surviving every power of the localized element remains nonzero,
whereas torsion after applying the comparison sends its fraction to zero.
The persistence and torsion facts are explicit premises. These proofs do
not certify the actual varieties, their birational invariant, RES descent,
or membership of the witness in the degree-zero piece.

No new accepted cited input has been added to the project. Any formal use
of the external threefolds would require an explicitly recorded source
interface rather than a global axiom. The retraction construction itself
needs the natural comparison, not its injectivity.
