## Audit of T001 (Proposition 3.18, `red:D:iso`): Ψ, μΨ not isomorphisms

Requested by the author. Independent audit (read-only), summarized here.

- Original text (from `reference/proposed-revisions/T001-approved.patch`):
  Ψ and μΨ "are both isomorphisms of graded semirings" (citing HK Cor. 10.3,
  Prop. 10.10(1)). Revised text: Ψ and μΨ are surjective with kernel
  congruences generated by coefficient-translation pairs.
- Verdict: the original claim is false over the paper's ground `C((t))`
  (verified). Example: `X = {s ∈ RV : s² = rv(t)}` (two non-definable points,
  value `1/2`). `[X]_0 ⊗ [(0,∞)]_1` and `[X]_0 ⊗ [(1/2,∞)]_1` have the same
  image under `(x,u) ↦ (x,xu)`, but are separated by an invariant of the Γ[1]
  presentation (translation by `1/2` is not a Γ-morphism, since the RV value
  group of the ground is `ℤ`). Same for μΨ (form `−id`, Γ-Jacobian `1/2`) and
  after groupification. T001's own detector (fifth roots of `rv(t)`) was also
  checked.
- The original statement holds when `Γ(dcl S) = vrv(RV(dcl S))`
  (e.g. VF-generated `S` with divisible value group); then the congruence is
  trivial.
- `C ⊆ ker` verified; `ker ⊆ C` plausible (manuscript sketch, spot-checked).
- Lean (`TensorPresentationContext.lean`): `proposition_3_18_unmeasured_kernel`,
  `proposition_3_18_measured_kernel` prove `congruence = RingCon.ker` over
  VF/Γ-generated grounds. **Gap:** no Lean result shows the kernel is
  nontrivial (no separating invariant, no concrete `C((t))` model facts); the
  Lean statements are equally true if the congruence were trivial.
- Downstream text still saying "isomorphism": introduction (lines ~645, ~663),
  Remark `eg:eb:factor` (~5840) and Section 8 `h_m` paragraph (~5927, false as
  written over `C((t))`). None is load-bearing; rewording needed.

## Remark 3.14 (divisible hull): repaired

`GammaDivisibleHull.lean`:
- `remark_3_14`: over a base generated (up to algebraic closure) by VF
  parameters `V`, a definable `γ ∈ Γ` has a nonzero power `γ^n` equal to the
  value of a nonzero element of the field generated by `V`, i.e. `γ` lies in
  the divisible hull of `Γ(𝕊)`. Proof: `γ^♯` is a nonempty definable set, so
  it meets `acl(V)` (an elementary substructure,
  `elementary_meets_definable_tuple`); the algebraic RV point lifts to the
  algebraically closed field `acl(V)` (`rv_algebraicClosure_iff_lift`); two
  maximal terms of the equation of the lift give the power
  (`exists_pow_value_of_isAlgebraic`).
- `remark_3_14_converse`: roots of definable values are definable.
- `remark_3_14_point`: over a VF-generated field base, a base point of `γ^♯`
  forces `γ` to be a value of the base field; so a definable `γ` outside
  `Γ(𝕊)` has no definable point in `γ^♯` (the second sentence).
Axioms: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

## Proposition 3.18: nontriviality witness (repaired, November 18)

The kernel of the unmeasured Ψ is nontrivial; this formalizes the audit's
counterexample to the original "isomorphism" claim. Placed in section 4
(it uses section 4's finite-boundary theory of definable unary Γ sets):
- `section 4/GermJumps.lean` (order theory only): one-sided germs, weighted
  germ jumps; additivity on disjoint tame sets, vanishing on finite sets,
  transport by order-preserving and order-reversing bijections.
- `section 4/GammaEndpointInvariant.lean`: definable unary Γ sets over the
  complete Γ ground are tame (`tame_line`); the odd weight `chi T δ`
  (`+1` on `δT`, `-1` on `δ⁻¹T`, `T` = subgroup generated by values of
  ground-definable RV units); `objNu_iso`: the endpoint invariant of degree-one
  Γ objects is invariant under Γ isomorphisms (via the piecewise unimodular
  classification `gammaGroundGammaHom_piecewiseGLZ`).
- `section 4/Proposition318Witness.lean`: a balanced detector
  `detector : K₊RES[*] ⊗ K₊Γ[*] →+* ℤ[ε]` (RES: degree-zero point count;
  Γ: degree-zero point count plus endpoint invariant times `ε`); the two sides
  agree on finite Γ coefficients (`detector_square`).
  `proposition_3_18_unmeasured_kernel_nontrivial`: for a nonempty RES[0]
  coefficient `X` of constant definable value `δ` with `δ² ∉ T`, the
  elementary pair `[X]⊗[(1,∞)]`, `[X]⊗[(δ,∞)]` has equal Ψ-images but distinct
  members; corollaries `gammaGroundUnmeasuredTensorMap_not_injective`,
  `coefficientCongruence_ne_bot`.
- `section 4/Proposition318CubeRoots.lean`:
  `proposition_3_18_kernel_nontrivial_of_noncube`: if a ground-definable RV
  unit `r₀` has a value that is not the cube of any element of `T`, its cube
  roots give such an `X` (nonempty, finite, constant value `v(r₀)^{1/3}`).
  Over `ℂ((t))` this applies to `r₀ = rv(t)` (the translation constants have
  integer values); constructing that model is not formalized.
Hypotheses: the Γ-side invariance uses the Reichstein–Youssin input of
Remark 3.15 (as all Γ classification results do), plus saturation and
smallness of the ground. A cube root is used because the odd weight vanishes
on the class of a square root (`δ ≡ -δ` modulo `T`); the audit's square-root
example is separated by a different invariant, not formalized. Only the
unmeasured Ψ is treated; the measured μΨ case is not formalized. Axioms:
propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.
