## Positive statement

Let T+ and T_mu+ be the actual sources of the two positive tensor maps.
For a RES object X with its degree-r finite label, put

    Lambda_X={delta in Gamma_def : exists an S-definable c:X -> delta^sharp}.

Define C as the least semiring congruence generated by

    [X]_r tensor [U]_m ~ [X]_r tensor [U+delta e_i]_m,

for all actual RES[r] coefficients, Gamma[m] objects, m>=1, coordinates
i and delta in Lambda_X. The witness c is existential. The pair of
classes is independent of the chosen witness at a fixed value delta.

For the measured map, retain the measured RES coefficient and generate
C_mu by the pairs with Gamma source (U,sigma) and target

    (U+delta e_i, sigma_delta),
    sigma_delta(eta)=sigma(eta-delta e_i)-delta.

The proved Gamma-ground statement is that both maps are surjective, with kernel
congruences exactly C and C_mu, hence that their induced quotient maps
are isomorphisms of graded semirings. No additive cancellation, ideals
of differences or localization is part of this statement.

## Why the ray-only family is not a positive presentation

This answers the author's question whether U is still needed after
returning to semirings. At least bounded U must be retained: the unary
open-ray family does not generate the positive kernel congruence.
This does not prove that every Gamma arity is indispensable, nor settle
whether a larger one-variable bounded family could generate all cases.

There is a three-element semiring of empty, bounded-nonempty, and
unbounded flags. Addition takes the larger flag; the product is zero
if either factor is empty and otherwise unbounded if either factor
is unbounded. A RES class contributes its emptiness flag, since every
RES object is bounded. A Gamma class contributes its boundedness flag.
The maps agree on finite Gamma coefficients and give a flag on T+.

This flag reflects zero. Expand a positive tensor as a finite sum of
pure object tensors. Its flag is empty only when each product has an
empty factor, in which case every summand is zero. The unbounded
region therefore excludes zero, absorbs addition by arbitrary
elements, and absorbs multiplication by nonzero elements.

Collapse all unbounded elements of T+ to a single equivalence class,
and keep equality on bounded elements. This is a semiring congruence:
the preceding absorption properties check addition and multiplication,
and multiplication by zero gives equality. The general algebraic
construction is checked in `SemiringRegionCongruence.lean`, including
the theorem `ringConGen_eq_outside_region`. No additive cancellation
is assumed there. The concrete flag construction and Laurent-series
example are mathematical arguments, not complete Lean model exports.

Every ray-only elementary pair lies in this coarse congruence: a
nonempty RES coefficient times a ray is unbounded, and an empty
coefficient gives the identical pair 0,0. It follows that the congruence
generated by all ray-only pairs cannot identify two distinct bounded
tensor classes.

There are such bounded classes in the actual kernel. Over S=C((t)),
let R={r:r^5=rv(t)}, delta=1/5, U=(0,delta], V=(delta,2delta]. Then

    a=[R]_0 tensor [U]_1, b=[R]_0 tensor [V]_1

are bounded and Psi+(a)=Psi+(b), by (r,u) |-> (r,ru). The original
balanced endpoint detector, already defined on the positive tensor
semiring with values in Z[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), gives

    a |-> -5 epsilon, b |-> 5 epsilon.

Thus a != b in T+ and the ray-only congruence cannot relate them. This
is an explicit obstruction, not merely the observation that the signed
cone proof cannot be copied into a semiring. The same bounded example
with Gamma forms -id gives the measured version.
