# T001: the measured tensor kernel

Current export status: both positive kernel congruences and both actual
ring-kernel ideals are now proved. See the dated export section at the
end; earlier pending-work notes below preserve their historical status.

This answers the author's question about the measured case after the
unmeasured kernel calculation. It is a mathematical calculation over the
same fixed ground S in ACVF(0,0), using the manuscript's Gamma-valued
volume forms. The complete kernel equality is not yet an exported Lean
theorem. No HK result is used as a premise, and no approved main text is
changed.

## Conventions and the sign of the correction

For an RV object (X,f,omega), write its combined weight as

    A_X(x) = omega(x) + sum val(f(x)).

For a measured Gamma object (U,sigma), write

    lambda_U(gamma) = sigma(gamma) + sum gamma.

The measured morphism condition is equality of combined weights. This
is exactly the manuscript's Gamma-Jacobian convention: the Jacobian is
the sum of the output values minus the sum of the input values, not a
valuation of a determinant to be added a second time. In particular the
Gamma-Jacobian of translation by delta e_i is delta.

For t_delta(gamma)=gamma+delta e_i define

    U_delta = U+delta e_i,
    sigma_delta(eta) = sigma(eta-delta e_i)-delta.              (1)

Then

    lambda_(U_delta,sigma_delta)(gamma+delta e_i)
      = sigma(gamma)-delta+sum gamma+delta
      = lambda_(U,sigma)(gamma).                              (2)

In combined-weight notation, therefore, the new weight is just transported
by t_delta inverse. The subtraction of delta in (1) is essential when
using the paper's original volume forms.

## Generating presentation

Put

    T_mu+ = K+ muRES[*] tensor_(K+ muGamma^fin[*]) K+ muGamma[*],
    T_mu  = K  muRES[*] tensor_(K  muGamma^fin[*]) K  muGamma[*].

Take every collection of data:

* a measured RES[r] object Xbold=(X,f,omega);
* an S-definable Gamma point delta;
* an S-definable function c:X -> delta^sharp;
* a measured Gamma[m] object (U,sigma), with m>=1;
* a coordinate index i.

Define the homogeneous degree-r+m element

    g_mu(Xbold,delta,U,sigma,i) =
      [Xbold]_r tensor ([(U,sigma)]_m-[(U_delta,sigma_delta)]_m).

Then the complete mathematical presentation is

    ker(mu-Psi) = ( g_mu(Xbold,delta,U,sigma,i) : all such data ). (M)

As in the corrected unmeasured notation, the element is indexed by delta,
not by a choice of c. The existence of c is the admissibility condition
on delta and X. The witness is used to construct the measured bijection;
it is not an additional dependence of the class on the right-hand side.

At the positive level, replace this ideal of differences with the
congruence generated by the corresponding pairs

    [Xbold]_r tensor [(U,sigma)]_m
      ~ [Xbold]_r tensor [(U_delta,sigma_delta)]_m.             (M+)

The form on the RES factor is unchanged. There is no extra condition on
c involving that form. The shifted Gamma form compensates for the change
in value of the free RV coordinate. The family is not asserted minimal
or finitely generated, and X need not be finite.

## Every generator is measured

Keep the finite label f on X and the identity finite label on the Gamma
lift. The map

    F(x,u_1,...,u_m) = (x,u_1,...,c(x)u_i,...,u_m)

has inverse given by division by c(x). Its Gamma-Jacobian relative to the
product labels f x id is delta, since the f(x) coordinates are unchanged.
The target volume form at F(x,u) is

    omega(x)+sigma_delta(val(u)+delta e_i)
      = omega(x)+sigma(val(u))-delta.

Adding the Jacobian delta gives the source volume form. Equivalently (2)
makes both total combined weights A_X(x)+lambda_U(val(u)). Thus F is an
actual S-definable measured RV isomorphism and each generator lies in the
kernel. It is not necessary to name a point of delta^sharp over S.

## Why these generate all measured equalities

The unmeasured local rectangle argument gives, on a full-rank product
neighbourhood in total degree N and maximal free Gamma arity m,

    F(x,u) = (h(x),c(x)u^M),
    k(gamma) = M gamma + bold_delta,                           (3)

where h is an S-definable RES[N-m] bijection, M is unimodular integral,
and c:X_0 -> (RV^times)^m has a constant S-definable value tuple
bold_delta. Its image is exactly the product with Gamma factor k(U_0).
The detailed construction and the original-ground parameter bookkeeping
are in `TENSOR-KERNEL-PRESENTATION.md`. This uses the local angular and
generic monomial results, not an assumed HK comparison theorem.

The remaining measured issue is that h need not preserve the inherited
RES weights by itself. Write A and B for the combined weights of the two
RES factors, and lambda and nu for those of the Gamma factors. The actual
measured condition on the whole product rectangle is

    A(x)+lambda(gamma) = B(h(x))+nu(k(gamma)).                 (4)

For a nonempty rectangle this forces

    A(x)-B(h(x)) = nu(k(gamma))-lambda(gamma) = a              (5)

for a single S-definable Gamma point a. To prove constancy, compare any
two x's at the same gamma, and any two gammas at the same x. No definable
point of X_0 or U_0 need be chosen. The constant is S-definable because it
is the unique value of a nonempty S-definable singleton image.

There is already a coefficient for this weight shift. Let

    p_a = [(Gamma^0 point, volume a)]_0 in K+ muGamma^fin[0].

Its RES lift is a singleton in degree zero with volume a, and
p_a p_b=p_(a+b), p_a p_(-a)=1. These are genuine shared coefficients;
they do not require RV representatives of a. Add a to the target RES
form, obtaining Ybold^[a]. Equation (5) says that h is a measured RES
isomorphism from Xbold to Ybold^[a]. The existing balancing relation
then gives

    [Xbold] tensor [(U,lambda)]
      = [Ybold^[a]] tensor [(U,lambda)]
      = [Ybold] tensor [(U,lambda+a)],                        (6)

where these Gamma objects are written in combined-weight notation. Their
original volume forms are obtained by subtracting sum gamma.

By (5), nu(k(gamma))=lambda(gamma)+a. The parameter-free map M is already
an allowed Gamma morphism after transporting the combined weight. For
each coordinate translation, apply (M+) with the function

    c_i composed with h^(-1): Y_0 -> (bold_delta_i)^sharp.

This transports the combined weight without changing it. After all m
translations, the resulting Gamma object has exactly the target combined
weight nu, by (5). Together with (6), this proves equality for the matched
rectangle modulo (M+).

The finite-cover, disjoint refinement and induction on remaining Gamma
rank now proceed as in the unmeasured argument. Normalizing a lower-rank
Gamma factor is compatible with volume forms: after a unimodular change
of coordinates, separate a fixed coordinate b and use the shared finite
measured coefficient

    e_b^0 = [({b},volume -b)]_1.

Its combined weight is zero. The remaining Gamma factor receives the
original transported combined weight, with its own coordinate sum
subtracted to recover its volume form. This gives an actual measured
product isomorphism and absorbs b^sharp into the RES factor by the
original balancing relation, preserving total degree. It never chooses
an RV lift of b over S.

Consequently every remainder has smaller free Gamma arity, with all
volume forms retained. At arity zero absorb the finite measured Gamma
factor, including its form, into RES; the remaining map is a measured
RES isomorphism. Positive scissors assemble the matched pieces. This
proves (M+) without cancellation. Groupification and positive
surjectivity then give (M), by the same common-summand argument as in the
unmeasured calculation. The abstract algebraic groupification comparison
is already checked in `GroupifiedCongruences.lean` and
`CompletionTensor.lean`; the complete geometric assembly is not yet a
Lean export.

## The kernel is genuinely nonzero

For S=C((t)), val(t)=1, let

    delta=1/5, R={r:r^5=rv(t)}, I=(0,infinity), J=(delta,infinity).

Use R as a RES[0] object with volume zero. The difference

    Delta_mu = [(R,0)]_0 tensor
        ([(I,0)]_1-[(J,-delta)]_1)                            (7)

is a generator, with c(r)=r. On the corresponding RV products,
F(r,u)=(r,ru) has Jacobian delta, and the constant target form -delta
makes it measured. The two Gamma forms in (7) are not both zero.

Forgetting all volume forms gives a well-defined homomorphism

    Forget_T:T_mu -> T,

because the RES, Gamma, and finite-Gamma forgetful maps commute with the
coefficient inclusions, products, scissors and isomorphisms. It sends
Delta_mu to the nonzero unmeasured element Delta in
`TENSOR-COEFFICIENT-OBSTRUCTION.md`. Hence Delta_mu is nonzero. This proves
failure of measured tensor injectivity also after groupification; it is
not merely a failure of cancellation at the positive level.

There is a useful stronger structural observation. Equip every
unmeasured RV object (X,f) with the volume form

    omega^0(x) = -sum val(f(x)).

Its combined weight is identically zero. All unmeasured isomorphisms
become measured, and this construction respects products, scissors,
finite-Gamma lifts, RES and Gamma. It induces a ring homomorphism

    Z_T:T -> T_mu,   Forget_T composed with Z_T = id_T,

commuting with Psi and mu-Psi via the analogous zero-combined-weight map
on RV. Thus Z_T embeds the entire unmeasured kernel into the measured
kernel. For the root example its embedded nonzero element is

    [(R,0)]_0 tensor ([(I,-id)]_1-[(J,-id)]_1).

Here -id is the function gamma |-> -gamma on each respective domain.
This is another choice of forms, different from (7). It does not
assert that all measured generators arise by this embedding alone:
arbitrary transported Gamma forms are present in (M).

## A uniform degree-one annihilator

Let

    tau_mu = 1 tensor [({0},0)]_1
           = [(G_m,id,0)]_1 tensor 1.

Then

    tau_mu * ker(mu-Psi) = 0.                               (8)

For a generator with c:X -> delta^sharp, multiplication by c gives the
measured RES isomorphism

    Xbold x (0^sharp,id,0)
      -> Xbold x (delta^sharp,id,-delta).

Both extra factors have combined weight zero. Balance the latter
coefficient e_delta^0 to the Gamma side. The integral shear

    (alpha,gamma) |-> (alpha,gamma+alpha e_i)

on the slice alpha=delta is a measured Gamma isomorphism between

    e_delta^0 x (U,sigma)
      and e_delta^0 x (U_delta,sigma_delta):

its Jacobian is delta and the second Gamma form drops by delta, as in
(1). Balance back and use the RES isomorphism in reverse. This proves
tau_mu times each generating pair is already equal in T_mu+, and
tau_mu g_mu=0 in T_mu. It follows from (M) and surjectivity that

    mu-Psi[tau_mu^(-1)]

is an isomorphism of the corresponding localizations. The annihilator
has degree one. It is not the degree-zero unit, it has not been
cancelled in the original ring, and equality of the kernel with its
whole annihilator is not asserted.

## Review and formalization boundary

The mathematical answer is the form-corrected generating presentation
(M), with the explicit nonzero element (7) and annihilation (8). The
source conventions, the Jacobian sign, degree-zero weight-shift
balancing, fixed-coordinate normalization, and the lower-rank induction
have all been included rather than inferred by simply attaching zero
forms to the unmeasured example. The concrete Laurent-series detector,
full kernel equalities, and uniform compactness/scissor arguments have
not yet been completed in Lean. Existing local monomial, combined-weight
and surjectivity exports remain available. No Lean source or verification
receipt has been modified by this mathematical follow-up.
